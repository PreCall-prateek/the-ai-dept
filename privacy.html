Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy describes how the JARVIS Intelligence Android app and this static website handle information. Please review it before using the app. Replace the contact placeholder before publishing.

1. Information and permissions

JARVIS uses information only as needed to provide the feature you choose. Android will ask for permissions when needed; you can deny or revoke them in your device settings. Some features will then be unavailable.

  • Microphone and speech: The app listens for speech after you activate its voice service. Android’s speech-recognition provider processes speech to return a transcript; how that provider handles audio can depend on your device and settings. Assistant text may then be sent to the AI service described below.
  • Contacts and calls: Contact access is used on-device when you ask JARVIS to find or call a contact. Android handles the call. Do not use contact actions unless you intend them.
  • Location: Location is accessed for a location-related request, such as checking a location-dependent answer or preparing a location link. If you ask JARVIS to share a location, it may be included in a message or link you direct it to prepare.
  • Camera: The app can open the camera when you request it. In the current app version, captured photos are not analyzed by the AI feature.
  • Notifications: If you enable notification access and ask JARVIS to read or reply to a supported notification, the app may process the selected notification content on the device and use Android’s reply action.
  • App settings and history: Your profile settings, voice selection, and recent conversation history are stored on your device. Conversation history is set to expire after up to three days; you can clear it in the app.

2. Third-party services

When you use features that need an online service, relevant request data is sent through the JARVIS Cloudflare Worker to that provider. JARVIS does not promise that an external provider will retain or process data in a particular way; check each provider’s own policy.

  • AI answers: Your message and the conversation context needed to answer it are sent to OpenRouter and the model provider selected by its routing. OpenRouter states that model providers can have different data practices. OpenRouter Privacy Policy.
  • Live web search: Some questions that appear to need current information are sent to Tavily as a search query. Tavily Privacy Policy.
  • Spoken replies and custom voices: When Fish Audio voice output is enabled, reply text is sent to Fish Audio to generate speech. If you choose to import a voice sample, that sample is sent to Fish Audio for the requested voice feature. Do not submit another person’s voice without their permission. Fish Audio Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting and security: Cloudflare processes requests to the JARVIS Worker to provide the app’s AI and voice proxy. Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising

The Android app includes Google Mobile Ads SDK integration and may show ads. The SDK may automatically collect or share information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Ad availability and personalization depend on region, device settings, and applicable consent choices. See Google’s AdMob data disclosure and Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Storage and retention

Conversation history is stored locally on your device and configured to expire after up to three days. You can clear it in the app. Requests sent to OpenRouter, model providers, Tavily, Fish Audio, Cloudflare, Google Mobile Ads, Android speech recognition, or other platforms may be subject to their own retention and deletion practices. JARVIS does not control those practices.

This website is a static GitHub Pages site. It does not include analytics, advertising scripts, or tracking pixels. GitHub may process technical information as part of hosting and serving the site; see GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

5. Your choices and safety

You can deny app permissions, turn off notification access, clear local history, or stop using online features. Avoid including passwords, payment details, highly sensitive personal data, or private information about someone else in AI prompts. AI-generated responses may be wrong; verify important information before acting on it.

6. Children

JARVIS is a general-purpose assistant and is not designed specifically for children. A parent or guardian should supervise use where required by local law.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features or providers change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when that happens. Material changes should be reviewed before continuing to use the app.
